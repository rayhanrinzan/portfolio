An internship recruiting simulator written in LOLCODE, because I was a little bored. It screens your experience and GPA, runs a technical interview where the right answer to "production is down" is to blame the intern, and then rejects you, with a 100% rejection rate. A perfect score gets flagged as AI-generated; anything less misses the required 101% match.
