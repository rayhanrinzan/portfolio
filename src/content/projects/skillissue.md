I was a little bored, so I wrote the recruiting pipeline in LOLCODE. You enter your name, years of experience and GPA, then sit a three-question technical interview: 2 + 2, the time complexity of binary search, and what to do when production is down (the correct answer is to blame the intern). An "AI-powered evaluation" then consults the hiring manager's nephew. The hard part was the scoring model. Zero years of experience means you're a beginner, ten or more means you're overqualified, and anything in between falls short of the ten years an entry-level role requires. A 4.0 GPA is too academically focused, and anything lower misses the required 4.0. The result is a 100% rejection rate, hard-coded. A perfect ATS score gets your application flagged as AI-generated, 75% or more shows too much potential, and anything lower misses the required 101% match.
